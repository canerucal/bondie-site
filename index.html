Play with peace of mind

A short adult check before every activity.

Adult supervision

Every activity should be chosen, adapted, and supervised by a responsible adult.

Notes on each card

Every game has a shield button. It opens the notes for that game: what is specific to it, and the reminders that apply. Read them before you start.

Space check

Remove sharp objects, slippery surfaces, cables, and tripping risks.

Material check

Use age-appropriate materials; check small parts, batteries, magnets, and allergens.

Age and development

Assess suggestions based on the child's age, development, interests, and readiness that day.

Children under 3

Young children put things in their mouths. Keep small objects, balloons, and anything that can come apart out of reach, and stay within arm's length.

Water, food, and outdoors

Never leave a child alone near water, even a shallow bowl. Check for allergies before games with food and cut food into small pieces. Outdoors, choose a spot away from traffic and watch the sun, heat, and cold.

Adapt and stop

If the child seems tired, overwhelmed, uninterested, or unsafe, stop the activity.

Health and emergencies

If injury, allergic symptoms, or health risks appear, stop the activity and seek appropriate professional help.

What data does Bondie store?

Bondie stores parent name, child name, child age, avatars, activity preferences, favorites, recently viewed games, and language preference in app storage on the device. This information is entered by the parent in the app or created locally while using the app. Bondie uses it to personalize the experience, show age- and preference-aware activity suggestions, and remember favorites and the games you looked at recently.

Where is data kept?

Bondie does not use an external database for this profile and activity data. This data is not sent off the device; the Bondie team and third-party service providers cannot access this local data.

Third-party sharing

Bondie does not share in-app profile or activity data with third parties for advertising, tracking, analytics, or marketing. The app does not use this data to track users across different apps or websites.

Support and contact data

When you email us for support, your email address and message reach us. This information is used only to answer your request, review privacy or safety reports, and follow up on bug feedback.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data is kept on the device while the user continues using the app. When the app is deleted, this data may be removed by the operating system. Bondie does not keep a separate server-side account or backup for this data. Emails you send us are kept in our support mailbox so we can respond to your request. These records are used only for support, privacy, safety reports, or bug feedback follow-up. For privacy questions, deletion of support records, or help deleting local data, write to hello@brocan.co.

What does Bondie not collect?

The current app experience does not ask for a child's email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, microphone, or camera. Bondie does not sell personal information.
